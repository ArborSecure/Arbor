5. Safety, trust, and moderation

Arbor is end-to-end encrypted and zero-knowledge, so we cannot read, scan, monitor, or moderate the content of messages, calls, or attachments. For the same reason, Arbor has no in-app "report content" feature: anything reported would reach us only as ciphertext we have no way to open, so we could not review or act on it.

Because of this, your safety on Arbor depends on who you let in. Every connection begins with an invitation, so treat inviting or accepting someone the way you would treat handing them a key to a private room. Only connect with people you know and trust, share invite links carefully, and remove anyone you no longer trust (use the Prune feature in the Network Tab).

A network's owner decides some of what members can see. In particular, if the owner turns on Monitoring, members can read the circle conversations of the people up to two levels below them, and receive copies of announcements made below them. The Privacy Policy (Section 3) explains exactly who can read what.

Parents and guardians should supervise any minor who uses Arbor, since neither we nor any automated filter can see or screen what is sent or received. You are responsible for the people you invite and interact with, and you use the Service at your own risk.

7. Plans, payments, renewal and cancellation

Free plan. A network on the free plan can have up to 20 members, with the history, storage and attachment limits shown in the app's plan panel.

Premium costs US$20 per month for one network (or personal hub) and is paid by that network's owner. It raises the member limit (to 2,000 members) and the history, storage and attachment limits as shown in the plan panel when you buy. Features listed there as "coming" are plans, not promises, and are not part of what you pay for until they ship.

Card payments renew automatically. Paying by card (through Stripe) starts a subscription that charges US$20 to your card at the start of each monthly period until you cancel it.

Cancel any time in the app: open the network's Network Plan panel and choose Cancel card subscription. It stops the next renewal; Premium stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you can undo the cancellation until then. Deleting the network cancels its subscription at once (the network is only deleted once the cancellation succeeds); deleting your account cancels it too, and if Stripe can't be reached at that moment we keep retrying for up to 30 days.

Monero payments (through NOWPayments) do not renew: each payment adds 30 days of Premium.

Price changes. If the price changes, we will tell you in the app before it applies to your next renewal, so you can cancel first.

Refunds. Unless required by law, payments and donations are non-refundable, and cancelling does not refund the current period. If a payment is refunded or disputed, the Premium days it paid for are removed.

If Premium ends. If a network has more members than the free plan allows when its Premium ends, it becomes archived 24 hours later: members can still open it and see its history, but no one can send messages, react, or add members until the owner renews, which restores it. Archiving itself deletes nothing, but once a network is no longer Premium, its messages older than 90 days are deleted from the server under the normal retention rules (copies on members' devices are not affected).

Payments are processed by Stripe and NOWPayments under their own terms. We never receive your card number or wallet details.
